# Version 3.3.0 — 27 September 2026

The preprint and repository now report one newly executed, auditable study. Historical manuscript numbers were replaced rather than labeled reproduced.

- Corrected exact ballot ties, optional random streams and neighborhood softmax underflow.
- Removed perfect type screening from default lottery eligibility; retained screening as a sensitivity and added uniform lottery.
- Added population aggregation, policy-weight ablation and matched clean-record ballots.
- Executed 55 conditions, 30 runs each, including a paired shock control; archived period traces and run-based estimates.
- Replaced political-superiority and adaptation claims with measured tradeoffs, analytical invariance and timing failures.
- Corrected four-archetype attribution and bounded-confidence novelty claims; disclosed substantive AI assistance.
- Added ODD description, all settings, manuscript sources, PDF, DOCX and citation metadata.
