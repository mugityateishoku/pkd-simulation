# Version 3.4.0 — 4 October 2026

Preprint version 2, **Accuracy and influence in councils selected by track record and lottery**, distinguishes nominal council membership from the coefficients used to form the decision. The September simulator and execution script remain unchanged.

- Added an exploratory 11-setting, 30-run follow-up with non-mutating observation of council IDs, selection roles, final weights and original-estimate coefficients. Four settings repeat September configurations with the same seeds and are not independent replications.
- Found that lottery members hold half the baseline seats but receive 13.26% of the input coefficients, or 12.27% after period 30. Added matched score/equal comparisons at five council compositions and finite-run participation measures.
- Reanalyzed archived delay and component comparisons by startup and later periods. Replaced persistent-penalty implications with the observed concentration near initialization, without claiming later equivalence.
- Corrected the September experiment count to 55 labeled batches and 54 distinct configurations. Verified that the duplicate setting's output tables are identical.
- Isolated the composition comparison by matching deliberation and final weights; documented score-scale, dimension and memory couplings.
- Added an independent audit of all 99,000 new coefficient rows, raw-to-summary calculations, reported intervals, matching and source hashes; the test suite now has 21 tests.
- Updated manuscript sources, PDF, DOCX, citation metadata and reproducibility instructions. No arXiv acceptance or publication DOI is claimed.

# Version 3.3.0 — 27 September 2026

The preprint and repository now report one newly executed, auditable study. Historical manuscript numbers were replaced rather than labeled reproduced.

- Corrected exact ballot ties, optional random streams and neighborhood softmax underflow.
- Removed perfect type screening from default lottery eligibility; retained screening as a sensitivity and added uniform lottery.
- Added population aggregation, policy-weight ablation and matched clean-record ballots.
- Executed 55 labeled batches, 30 runs each, including a paired shock control; archived period traces and run-based estimates. The October audit identified one duplicated setting, leaving 54 distinct configurations.
- Replaced political-superiority and adaptation claims with measured tradeoffs, analytical invariance and timing failures.
- Corrected four-archetype attribution and bounded-confidence novelty claims; disclosed substantive AI assistance.
- Added ODD description, all settings, manuscript sources, PDF, DOCX and citation metadata.
